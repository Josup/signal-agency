## Editorial policy

The Signal Journal publishes explanatory and how-to writing about AI search. Every
article passes a deterministic publish gate before it ships. The gate rejects
invented client counts, case studies about businesses that are not clients, and any
first-party research claim Signal did not actually run. Figures in our articles come
from a maintained approved-facts table or from a named, attributed public source.
