What actually happens

1

We run your customers' prompts, live

"Best optometrist in Crown Heights." "BJJ private lessons Brooklyn." Whatever your customers would type, we type — into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews — while you watch. Whoever gets named, gets named in front of you.

Questions, answered

What does the free AI search audit include?

On a 20-minute call, we run the prompts your customers actually type into ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews — live, while you watch — and see who gets named. Then we look at why: your website's structured data, your Google Business Profile, your citations, and how retrievable your pages are to AI crawlers. You get a written audit within 48 hours.

How much does the audit cost?

Nothing. The audit is free, and it is not a disguised sales deck — it's the same diagnostic Signal runs at the start of every paid engagement. If the audit shows you don't need help, it says so.

Do I need a website to take the audit?

No. If you don't have a website yet, the audit shows what AI engines currently say about your category and neighborhood, which competitors are being cited, and what a citable web presence for your business would need to include.

What happens after the audit?

You keep the written audit either way. If the findings point to work worth doing, Signal offers a scoped proposal — a website build from $2,500 or a monthly retainer at $500/month. There is no obligation and no follow-up sequence.

Is this a sales call?

No. The 20 minutes are spent running your prompts and reading the results. Pricing only comes up if you ask, or if the written audit recommends work — and the audit is yours to keep regardless.
