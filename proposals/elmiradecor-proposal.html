AI Visibility — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI

When someone asks ChatGPT "who is the best interior designer in Brooklyn," Elmira Decor doesn't appear. The site has no FAQ content, no quoted expert statements, and no substantive passages an AI model can cite. The only blog post contains Wix template placeholder text — zero real content.

22
Local Search — "Interior designer Brooklyn"

No physical address is published on the website. No Google Business Profile signals were detected. The contact page has a phone number only. Without NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency and location schema, the site cannot rank for local searches in Brooklyn or NYC.

18
Content Optimization — Telling the full story

The portfolio page contains zero text — it links out to Instagram. Service descriptions are one-liners with no explanations. The about page doesn't include a last name. 650 words of content across 8 pages is not enough for search engines or AI tools to form a clear picture of what you do, who you serve, or why you're exceptional.

25
Schema Markup — Can AI tools understand the business?

Zero structured data detected across all pages. No LocalBusiness schema, no InteriorDesigner type, no Review markup, no Service definitions. This means AI models and search engines are guessing at what the site is about rather than reading clear machine-readable signals. This is the single largest technical gap.

5
Google Business Profile

No GBP embed, no map, no consistent business address — GBP status could not be confirmed. The phone number also differs between the website (305 area code) and Houzz (727 area code), which creates trust and tracking confusion for both Google and prospective clients.

—
Mobile Performance

The Wix site is mobile-responsive — this is a genuine strength. AI crawlers are not blocked (robots.txt is correctly configured). However, Wix's JavaScript-first rendering means AI crawlers without JS execution receive mostly empty HTML, limiting how much content they can actually index.

35
Blog and Content Presence

One blog post exists, dated May 2021. It is entirely Wix template instructions — not a single sentence of real interior design content. The blog categories sitemap exists but has no active posts. A site with no content pipeline has no mechanism for organic search growth over time.

    Houzz, Instagram, and TikTok are strong — but they're rented land. Algorithm changes, platform policies, or a single suspension can eliminate reach overnight. An owned website with strong SEO is the only durable, compounding asset in your digital presence.
  • 3
