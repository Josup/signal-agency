How it works

Send a two-line email to josh@signalai.agency with the business name and the owner's name, and copy them in if they're expecting it. That's the whole process. Or forward them this page and let them reach out themselves — just tell me it was you.

They get the same thing anyone gets: a free audit of how their business shows up when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI for a business like theirs, with the findings in writing. If the findings point to work worth doing, I'll propose it. If they don't, I'll say so, and you've still done them a favour.

Who's a good fit

  • An independent local business with a real storefront or service area — an optometrist, a gym, a dentist, a contractor, a salon, an accountant.
  • An owner who cares whether new customers can find them, and hasn't touched the website in a while.
  • Anywhere in the US. Brooklyn is home, but the work is the same everywhere.

Who isn't

  • Franchises and multi-location chains with a marketing department.
  • Anyone who wants a promise of a ranking or a number of leads. The evidence page shows what Signal records and how; nothing on it is a forecast.

The fine print, all of it

  • The fee is paid when the referred business's first invoice is paid, whether that's a build or a retainer.
  • One fee per business, regardless of how many people mention them. First introduction on record wins.
  • Existing Signal clients and businesses already in conversation with Signal don't count — you'd know if you were one.
  • Signal can change or end the programme; anything already earned is still paid.
